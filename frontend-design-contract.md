# Frontend design contract

Countermelody is a split listening room built around a rotating relationship diagram. It is not a workflow dashboard, a journey, a lane board, a card grid, or an ordered progress surface. Two asymmetrical writing fields face each other across an orbital score figure. One horizontal intent strip triggers a single analysis. The result reads as a collectible record seal with three binary listening checks and two content fingerprints. Coal, ivory, acid chartreuse, and rust replace the old nocturne palette. Large editorial type and concentric motion replace stages, ticks, seats, and playheads.
