# Countermelody identity

- Mechanism signature: one-shot semantic comparison of two authored musical lines.
- Product metaphor: a mastering-room listening note, not an ensemble rehearsal.
- Narrative voice: critical, compact, and written like liner notes.
- Visual grammar: bilateral writing surfaces orbiting one relationship diagram.
- Interaction grammar: enter two voices, state one intent, receive one immutable comparison.
- Result grammar: record seal, three verified listening checks, bounded friction flags, and paired digests.
- Evidence story: deployed source, finalized analysis receipt, stored structured result, and public readback.

The contract has no seats, route segments, participant queue, success counter, strike counter, or incremental completion state.
